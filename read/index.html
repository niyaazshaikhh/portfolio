02 A note for your reader

Choose a format
that feels right.

EPUB reflows to fit an e-reader or phone. PDF keeps a fixed page layout, which is useful for textbooks and illustrated works.

Availability and download options depend on each book’s rights and the source’s terms. For newer titles, try your local library or the author’s and publisher’s official pages.
